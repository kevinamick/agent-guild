# Sound credits

- `office-chatter.mp3`: "Office chatter and typing.wav" by Alex_hears_things,
  https://freesound.org/s/636861/, released under Creative Commons 0 (public domain). A field recording of a
  real office (voices, keyboards, mice). Peaks gently compressed, cut into a seamless ~3 min 20 s loop and
  loudness-normalized with ffmpeg. Attribution isn't required; given with thanks.

All other sounds are generated in the browser (client/src/audio/synth.js).
